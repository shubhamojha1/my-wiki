# Martin Fowler

**Type:** Software engineer, author, speaker  
**Website:** [martinfowler.com](https://martinfowler.com/)  
**Organization:** Thoughtworks (Chief Scientist)

## Overview

Martin Fowler is a prominent figure in software engineering, known for his work on refactoring, enterprise architecture, microservices, continuous delivery, and domain-driven design. He maintains the bliki (blog + wiki) at martinfowler.com, which covers a wide range of software development topics.

## Key Contributions

- **Refactoring** — Author of *Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code* (1999, 2nd ed. 2018) with Kent Beck
- **Enterprise Architecture** — *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture* (2002)
- **Microservices** — Co-author of the seminal article *Microservices* (2014) with James Lewis
- **Continuous Delivery** — Co-author of the book with Jez Humble
- **Bliki** — Pioneered the blog+wiki hybrid format for software knowledge

## Notable Quotes

Popularized Phil Karlton's famous quote on [[Cache Invalidation]]: "There are only two hard things in Computer Science: cache invalidation and naming things."
