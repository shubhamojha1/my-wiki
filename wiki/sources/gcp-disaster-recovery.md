# Google Cloud: What Is Disaster Recovery?

**URL:** https://cloud.google.com/learn/what-is-disaster-recovery

## Summary

A comprehensive introductory guide to disaster recovery (DR) covering definitions, DR planning steps, types of DR, key metrics (RTO/RPO), the 3-2-1 backup rule, and the role of cloud in modern DR strategies.

## Key Concepts Covered

- **Definition**: Restoring IT infrastructure after a disaster event (subset of business continuity)
- **5 DR steps**: Risk assessment → Business impact analysis → DR planning → Implementation → Testing & maintenance
- **3-2-1 backup rule**: 3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite
- **Types**: Backups, BaaS, DRaaS, point-in-time snapshots, virtual DR, DR sites
- **3 recovery elements**: Preventive (avoid disaster), Detective (detect in real time), Corrective (restore)
- **Key metrics**: RTO (max downtime tolerable), RPO (max data loss tolerable)
- **Benefits**: data protection, business continuity, reduced financial impact, compliance, reputation
