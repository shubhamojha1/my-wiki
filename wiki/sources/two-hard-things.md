# Two Hard Things

**Source:** Martin Fowler's bliki (July 2009, updated through 2021)

Documents the origin and variations of the famous quote by Phil Karlton:

> *"There are only two hard things in Computer Science: cache invalidation and naming things."*

## History

- Phil Karlton originally said this around 1996–1997
- First found on the internet in Tim Bray's blog (2005)
- Tim Bray recalls hearing it around 1996–1997

## Notable Variations

| Variation | Author |
|-----------|--------|
| Adds "off-by-1 errors" as a third hard thing | Leon Bambrick |
| Distributed systems: exactly-once delivery and guaranteed order of messages | Mathias Verraes |
| Meta-joke: "we only have one joke and it's not funny" | Phillip Scott Bowden |
| Observation about how many variations exist | Nat Pryce |
