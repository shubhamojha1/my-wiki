# Startup Readiness Checklist

A startup readiness checklist is one of the concrete outputs of an [[Initialization Phase]]: a document confirming the project is actually ready for implementation work to begin — not just that setup steps were run, but that they were verified. It's produced alongside a runnable environment, a verifiable test framework, a task breakdown with acceptance criteria, and a git checkpoint commit.

The checklist matters because it's the artifact that turns "I ran the setup commands" into something checkable by a later session — the same principle behind [[Definition of Done]] and the [[Harness Engineering|Feedback Subsystem]], applied to the setup step itself rather than to a feature.
